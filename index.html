About McKenna

Personal guidance with market-minded strategy.

Hi, I am McKenna Thaggard, owner of McKenna & Co. Realty Group and an Atlanta Communities Realtor serving Atlanta and North Georgia.

I value upfront, honest communication and I like the process to feel personal, not transactional. Whether you are buying your first home, selling a long-loved property, or planning your next move, my job is to make sure you understand what is happening and why.

My double-major Bachelor of Science degree in economics and business shapes the way I work: I pay attention to pricing, market shifts, negotiation strategy, and the small details that help clients feel prepared instead of pressured.
